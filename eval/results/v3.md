### SH01 (hard, values)

Generated SQL:
```sql
SELECT 
    ROUND(SUM(
        s.seats * p.list_price * (1 - COALESCE(s.discount_pct, 0)) *

WHERE s.status = 'active'
  AND s.start_date <= '2025-12-31'
  AND (s.end_date IS NULL OR s.end_date > '2025-12-31')
  AND p.currency = 'USD'
```
